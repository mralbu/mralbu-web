Presented at the 2024 Rio Oil & Gas Conference, this work addresses relative permeability modeling of heterogeneous carbonate rocks from laboratory scale to field-scale reservoir simulation in a Brazilian presalt field case.

Awarded the **Plínio Cantanhede Award** for the best paper in the Upstream Section at Rio Oil & Gas 2024.
